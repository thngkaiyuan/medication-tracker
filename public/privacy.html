MedTracker Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

MedTracker is designed to keep your medication information private and under your control.

Data stored by the app

Medication names, schedules, daily dose limits, and dose timestamps are stored only on your device. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated server.

Data sharing

MedTracker does not transmit your medication data. Data leaves the app only when you intentionally use Export Data and choose a destination from the system share sheet. You control and are responsible for that destination.

Backups and deletion

You can export a JSON backup from the app. You can delete individual records or medications in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data unless it remains in a backup you created.

Web version

The optional web version uses local browser storage. Its hosting provider may process standard connection information under that provider's own privacy policy, but medication records remain in local browser storage and are not sent to the app developer.

Medical disclaimer

MedTracker is a personal record-keeping tool, not a medical device and not a substitute for professional medical advice. Follow the instructions of your clinician and pharmacist.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the MedTracker support repository.
