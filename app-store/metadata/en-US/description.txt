MedTracker is a simple, private way to record medication doses and see when the next dose is due.

• Add medications with custom intervals and optional daily limits
• Log a dose in a tap
• Review, add, edit, and delete dose-history entries
• Export and import portable JSON backups
• Use every tracking feature without an internet connection

Your medication data stays on your device. MedTracker has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

MedTracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend doses, or replace advice from your clinician or pharmacist.
