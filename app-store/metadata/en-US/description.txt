MedTracker is a simple, private way to record medication doses and see at a glance when the waiting limits you entered have cleared.

• Set a minimum interval and optional rolling 24-hour limit for each medication
• Log a dose in a tap
• Follow the color-coded wait as your entered limits approach
• Review, add, edit, and delete dose-history entries
• Export and import portable JSON backups
• Use every tracking feature without an internet connection

Your medication data stays on your device. MedTracker has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Colors reflect only the minimum interval and rolling 24-hour limit you enter. They do not determine whether a dose is medically safe. MedTracker does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend doses, or replace the medication label or advice from your clinician or pharmacist.
